# FOREWORD

The final command Jesus gave His followers before returning to heaven was simple yet profound:

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations..."  
> — Matthew 28:19

Throughout history, many people have called themselves Christians. Churches have been built, ministries established, and countless books written. Yet the question remains: What does it truly mean to follow Jesus?

Christianity is more than believing certain facts about God. It is more than attending church services. It is more than adopting a moral lifestyle.

Christianity begins with a relationship with Jesus Christ and grows through a lifelong journey of discipleship.

A disciple is a learner, a follower, and an apprentice of Jesus. Disciples seek to know Christ, obey His teachings, imitate His character, and participate in His mission.

This workbook was written to help believers understand the foundational truths of the Christian faith and to encourage spiritual growth through Bible study, reflection, discussion, and personal application.

Whether you are exploring Christianity for the first time, preparing for baptism, participating in a small group, or helping others grow in their faith, this guide is intended to help you build a strong biblical foundation.

The Christian life is not a sprint but a marathon. It is not merely about beginning well but about finishing faithfully.

Our prayer is that God will use these studies to deepen your understanding of His Word, strengthen your faith, transform your character, and equip you to make disciples of others.

May the Lord bless you as you seek Him and follow Him.
