# Appendix A
# How To Study The Bible

Many Christians want to study the Bible but are unsure where to begin.

A simple approach is:

## S.O.A.P. Method

### S — Scripture

Read the passage carefully.

Write down a key verse.

### O — Observation

What does the passage say?

Who is speaking?

What is happening?

What stands out?

### A — Application

What does this passage mean for my life?

What should I do differently?

### P — Prayer

Respond to God in prayer.

Thank Him.

Confess sin.

Ask for help in applying His Word.

---

Questions To Ask:

1. What does this passage teach about God?
2. What does it teach about people?
3. Is there a command to obey?
4. Is there a promise to believe?
5. Is there an example to follow?
6. Is there a warning to avoid?

Remember:

The goal of Bible study is not merely knowledge.

The goal is obedience and transformation.
