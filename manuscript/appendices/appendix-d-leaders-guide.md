# Appendix D
# Leader's Guide

## Purpose

This workbook is designed to facilitate spiritual growth and disciple-making.

The leader's role is not merely to teach information.

The leader's role is to help participants follow Jesus.

---

## Suggested Meeting Format

Opening Prayer
5 minutes

Review Previous Lesson
10 minutes

Teaching Discussion
30 minutes

Reflection Questions
20 minutes

Application Commitments
10 minutes

Prayer
15 minutes

Total:
Approximately 90 minutes

---

## Discussion Guidelines

Ask questions more than you lecture.

Encourage participation.

Keep Scripture central.

Focus on application.

Maintain confidentiality.

Pray for one another.

---

## Remember

Discipleship is relational.

People often learn more from your example than from your words.

Teach truth.

Model obedience.

Demonstrate love.

Encourage growth.

Pray consistently.
