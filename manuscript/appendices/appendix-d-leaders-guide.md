# Appendix D
# Leader's Guide

## Purpose

Foundations of Discipleship is designed to help believers:

- Know Christ
- Follow Christ
- Become like Christ
- Make disciples

This workbook is not merely a Bible study.

It is a discipleship tool.

The goal is not simply the transfer of information.

The goal is spiritual transformation through obedience to Jesus Christ.

As a leader, your responsibility is not merely to teach lessons.

Your responsibility is to help people become disciples who follow Jesus and help others follow Him.

---

## The Difference Between Teaching and Training

Imagine teaching someone how to play basketball.

You could provide books.

You could show videos.

You could explain the rules.

Yet no one becomes a basketball player merely by studying the game.

A player must practice, receive coaching, make mistakes, and continue growing through experience.

Discipleship works the same way.

People do not become disciples simply by gaining biblical knowledge.

They become disciples as they learn to obey what Jesus taught.

Teaching provides information.

Training produces transformation.

Whenever possible, help participants move beyond discussion and into practical obedience.

Ask:

- What did we learn?
- What should we do?
- How will we apply this?
- How can we help one another obey Christ?

---

## Suggested Meeting Format

### Opening Prayer
5 minutes

Invite God to guide the discussion and prepare hearts to receive His Word.

### Review Previous Lesson
10 minutes

Discuss previous commitments and applications.

Ask participants:

- What did you apply this week?
- What challenges did you face?
- What did God teach you?

### Teaching Discussion
30 minutes

Read key passages together.

Allow participants to discover truth from Scripture rather than simply receiving information from the leader.

### Reflection Questions
20 minutes

Encourage honest discussion.

Avoid rushing to answers.

Allow people to wrestle with the implications of God's Word.

### Application Commitments
10 minutes

Ask each participant to identify one specific action step for the coming week.

Application is where discipleship becomes transformation.

### Prayer
15 minutes

Pray for one another.

Pray specifically regarding the commitments made during the session.

Total Time:
Approximately 90 minutes

---

## Discussion Guidelines

### Ask More Questions Than You Answer

People often learn best when they discover truth for themselves.

### Keep Scripture Central

Opinions may be helpful, but Scripture must remain the final authority.

### Focus on Application

Always move beyond:

"What does this passage mean?"

to

"How should we respond?"

### Encourage Participation

Avoid allowing one person to dominate the discussion.

### Maintain Confidentiality

People should feel safe sharing personal struggles and questions.

### Pray Consistently

Spiritual growth is ultimately the work of the Holy Spirit.

---

## Helping People Grow

Remember that spiritual growth occurs at different rates.

Some participants may respond quickly.

Others may need more time.

Be patient.

Encourage progress rather than perfection.

Celebrate small steps of obedience.

Discipleship is often a long process of faithful investment.

---

## Accountability and Encouragement

Spiritual growth rarely occurs in isolation.

Encourage participants to support one another between meetings.

Consider pairing participants with accountability partners who can pray together, encourage one another, and discuss their application commitments during the week.

Accountability should never become legalistic or controlling.

Its purpose is encouragement, growth, and mutual support.

As disciples help one another follow Christ, spiritual growth often accelerates.

---

## Multiplication

The goal of discipleship is not merely mature believers.

The goal is disciples who make disciples.

Encourage participants to think beyond their own growth.

Ask:

- Who can I pray for?
- Who can I encourage?
- Who can I share the Gospel with?
- Who can I help follow Jesus?

Healthy disciples eventually become disciple-makers.

---

## A Final Encouragement to Leaders

People often learn more from your example than from your words.

Teach truth.

Model obedience.

Demonstrate humility.

Show love.

Walk with people patiently.

Depend upon the Holy Spirit.

Remember that Jesus did not simply command us to teach people everything He commanded.

He commanded us to teach them to obey everything He commanded.

May God use you to help others know Christ, follow Christ, become like Christ, and make disciples for the glory of God.
