# CHAPTER 10

# THE HOLY SPIRIT

## Key Verse

John 14:16–17

> "And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Helper, to be with you forever..."

* * *

# Introduction

Many Christians understand God the Father.

Many understand Jesus Christ the Son.

Yet the Holy Spirit is often the least understood person of the Trinity.

Some think of the Holy Spirit as a force.

Others view Him merely as an influence.

Still others focus only on spiritual gifts or supernatural experiences.

The Bible presents a much richer picture.

The Holy Spirit is not merely a power.

He is a person.

He is fully God.

He is the third Person of the Trinity.

The Christian life cannot be lived successfully through human effort alone.

God has given His Spirit to every believer.

The Holy Spirit convicts, teaches, guides, comforts, empowers, and transforms God's people.

Without the Holy Spirit, Christianity becomes a burden of religious activity.

With the Holy Spirit, Christianity becomes a living relationship with God.

To understand discipleship, we must understand the ministry of the Holy Spirit.

* * *

# 1\. WHO IS THE HOLY SPIRIT?

## Scripture

Acts 5:1–4

(Read the passage.)

The Holy Spirit is not an impersonal force.

He is a divine person.

Throughout Scripture, the Holy Spirit demonstrates characteristics of personhood.

He:

- Speaks
- Teaches
- Guides
- Loves
- Comforts
- Can be grieved

These are not qualities of an impersonal power.

They are qualities of a person.

The Holy Spirit is also fully God.

In Acts 5, Peter tells Ananias that lying to the Holy Spirit is equivalent to lying to God.

The Father is God.

The Son is God.

The Holy Spirit is God.

Yet there is only one God.

This truth forms part of the biblical doctrine of the Trinity.

While the Trinity remains a profound mystery, Scripture clearly teaches the full deity of the Holy Spirit.

Because He is God, the Holy Spirit deserves our worship, reverence, trust, and obedience.

# 2\. THE HOLY SPIRIT IN THE LIFE OF THE BELIEVER

## Scripture

Romans 8:9–11

(Read the passage.)

One of the most remarkable truths in the New Testament is that the Holy Spirit dwells within every believer.

In the Old Testament, the Spirit often came upon individuals for specific purposes.

In the New Testament, the Spirit permanently indwells believers.

This means that every Christian has direct access to God's presence.

The Holy Spirit is not reserved for pastors, missionaries, or spiritually mature believers.

Every Christian receives the Holy Spirit.

This truth has several important implications.

### We Are Never Alone

God is continually present with His people.

### We Belong To God

The Spirit's presence confirms that we are God's children.

### We Have Spiritual Power

Believers are empowered to live for Christ.

### We Can Grow Spiritually

Transformation becomes possible through the Spirit's work.

The Christian life is not merely imitation.

It is participation.

The Holy Spirit works within believers to produce Christlike character.

# 3\. THE HOLY SPIRIT CONVICTS OF SIN

## Scripture

John 16:7–11

(Read the passage.)

Before people can receive the Gospel, they must recognize their need for it.

One of the Holy Spirit's primary ministries is conviction.

Conviction is different from condemnation.

### Condemnation

Condemnation says:

"You are hopeless."

"There is no forgiveness."

"There is no way back."

### Conviction

Conviction says:

"This is wrong."

"You need forgiveness."

"Come back to God."

The Holy Spirit lovingly exposes sin so that people can experience repentance and restoration.

Without conviction, people remain spiritually blind.

Many believers can remember a time when they suddenly became aware of their sinfulness and their need for Christ.

This awareness was not merely human insight.

It was the work of the Holy Spirit.

Even after conversion, the Spirit continues to convict believers when they wander from God's will.

His goal is not shame.

His goal is restoration.

# 4\. THE HOLY SPIRIT TEACHES AND GUIDES

## Scripture

John 14:26

(Read the passage.)

Jesus promised that the Holy Spirit would teach His followers and remind them of His words.

The Holy Spirit helps believers understand Scripture.

This does not eliminate the need for study.

Rather, it enables believers to grasp spiritual truth.

The Spirit illuminates God's Word.

He helps us:

- Understand truth
- Apply truth
- Discern error
- Grow in wisdom

The Holy Spirit never guides believers contrary to Scripture.

Because He inspired Scripture, He always works in harmony with Scripture.

Many Christians seek guidance regarding:

- Decisions
- Relationships
- Ministry
- Career
- Service

God often provides direction through:

- Scripture
- Prayer
- Wise counsel
- Circumstances
- The inward prompting of the Spirit

Discerning God's guidance requires humility, patience, and a willingness to obey.

The Spirit guides those who desire to follow Christ.

# 5\. THE HOLY SPIRIT COMFORTS AND STRENGTHENS

## Scripture

John 14:16

(Read the passage.)

Jesus referred to the Holy Spirit as the Helper.

Different Bible translations use terms such as:

- Helper
- Comforter
- Counselor
- Advocate

Each captures part of the Spirit's ministry.

The Christian life includes:

- Trials
- Suffering
- Disappointment
- Loss
- Persecution

Believers are not promised an easy life.

They are promised God's presence.

The Holy Spirit strengthens believers during difficult times.

He provides:

- Comfort in sorrow
- Peace in uncertainty
- Courage in fear
- Hope in suffering

Many believers can testify that during their darkest moments they experienced God's sustaining presence in a remarkable way.

This is one of the ministries of the Holy Spirit.

He walks with God's people through every season of life.

# 6\. THE FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT

## Scripture

Galatians 5:22–23

(Read the passage.)

One of the clearest evidences of the Holy Spirit's work is the transformation of character.

Many people focus on spiritual gifts.

The New Testament places equal, if not greater, emphasis on spiritual fruit.

Gifts demonstrate what a believer can do.

Fruit demonstrates who a believer is becoming.

Paul identifies nine characteristics that the Holy Spirit produces in the lives of believers.

### Love

Love is the foundation of Christian character.

Biblical love seeks the good of others and reflects God's own love.

### Joy

Christian joy is deeper than happiness.

Happiness depends upon circumstances.

Joy rests in God's unchanging goodness.

### Peace

The Holy Spirit produces inner stability and confidence in God even during difficult circumstances.

### Patience

Patience enables believers to endure difficulties and deal graciously with imperfect people.

### Kindness

Kindness reflects God's compassion toward others.

### Goodness

Goodness involves moral integrity and a desire to do what is right.

### Faithfulness

Faithfulness produces reliability, loyalty, and perseverance.

### Gentleness

Gentleness combines strength with humility and self-control.

### Self-Control

Self-control enables believers to govern desires, emotions, and actions according to God's will.

Notice that Paul refers to the "fruit" of the Spirit, not the "fruits."

These qualities work together as aspects of a Christlike life.

The goal of the Christian life is not merely activity.

The goal is transformation into the likeness of Jesus Christ.

# 7\. WALKING BY THE SPIRIT

## Scripture

Galatians 5:16–18

(Read the passage.)

The Christian life involves a daily choice.

Will we follow the desires of the flesh or the leading of the Spirit?

Paul instructs believers to:

> "Walk by the Spirit."

Walking suggests a continuous lifestyle rather than an occasional experience.

To walk by the Spirit means to live under His influence and guidance.

It involves:

- Listening to God's Word
- Responding to conviction
- Obeying God's commands
- Depending upon God's strength

Walking by the Spirit does not mean perfection.

Believers still face temptations and struggles.

However, the direction of life changes.

Instead of being controlled by sinful desires, believers increasingly submit themselves to God.

The Christian life is not primarily about trying harder.

It is about surrendering more fully to God's work within us.

As believers walk with the Spirit, spiritual fruit grows naturally.

Healthy fruit is not manufactured.

It develops through a healthy relationship with the source of life.

# 8\. SPIRITUAL GIFTS

## Scripture

1 Corinthians 12

(Read the chapter.)

The Holy Spirit not only transforms believers internally but also equips them for service.

Spiritual gifts are abilities given by God for building up the Church and advancing His mission.

Scripture identifies many different gifts, including:

- Teaching
- Encouragement
- Leadership
- Service
- Giving
- Mercy
- Evangelism
- Shepherding
- Wisdom
- Discernment

Not every believer receives the same gifts.

The Holy Spirit distributes gifts according to His wisdom and purposes.

This diversity is beneficial.

A healthy church requires many different kinds of service.

Spiritual gifts should never become a source of pride.

They are gifts, not achievements.

They were given to serve others rather than elevate ourselves.

The proper question is not:

"What gift do I have?"

The deeper question is:

"How can I use what God has given me to serve others and glorify Christ?"

# 9\. THE HOLY SPIRIT AND THE CHURCH

## Scripture

Ephesians 4:1–16

(Read the passage.)

The Holy Spirit works not only in individual believers but also within the Church as a whole.

The Spirit creates unity among believers.

He equips leaders.

He distributes gifts.

He empowers ministry.

He strengthens the Church's witness.

Without the Holy Spirit, the Church becomes merely a human organization.

With the Holy Spirit, the Church becomes a living expression of Christ's presence in the world.

Throughout the book of Acts, we repeatedly see the Spirit:

- Directing mission
- Empowering evangelism
- Guiding decisions
- Strengthening believers
- Producing spiritual growth

The Church grows not merely through human effort but through the power of the Holy Spirit.

This truth should encourage humility and dependence upon God.

# 10\. GRIEVING THE HOLY SPIRIT

## Scripture

Ephesians 4:30

> "And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God..."

Because the Holy Spirit is a person, our actions matter to Him.

Paul warns believers not to grieve the Spirit.

The context of this command includes attitudes and behaviors such as:

- Bitterness
- Anger
- Slander
- Malice
- Dishonesty
- Unforgiveness

When believers persist in sin, they hinder their fellowship with God and resist the Spirit's work.

This does not mean the Spirit abandons believers.

Rather, it means that sin damages intimacy and spiritual vitality.

The solution is repentance.

When believers confess sin and return to God, fellowship is restored.

The goal is not guilt.

The goal is a life that honors God and remains sensitive to His leading.

# 11\. BEING FILLED WITH THE SPIRIT

## Scripture

Ephesians 5:18

(Read the passage.)

Paul commands believers to be filled with the Spirit.

Unlike the Spirit's indwelling, which occurs when a person becomes a believer, being filled with the Spirit describes ongoing surrender and empowerment.

To be filled with the Spirit means to be controlled by the Spirit.

A Spirit-filled believer increasingly displays:

- Worship
- Gratitude
- Humility
- Obedience
- Love
- Service

Being filled with the Spirit is not primarily about unusual experiences.

It is about living under God's influence.

As believers continually yield themselves to God, the Spirit produces Christlike character and empowers effective ministry.

The goal of the Christian life is not merely to possess the Spirit.

It is to allow the Spirit to possess more of us.

# CHAPTER SUMMARY

The Holy Spirit is the third Person of the Trinity and fully God.

He dwells within every believer and plays an essential role in the Christian life.

The Spirit:

- Convicts of sin
- Teaches truth
- Guides believers
- Provides comfort
- Produces spiritual fruit
- Distributes spiritual gifts
- Strengthens the Church
- Empowers ministry

Believers are called to walk by the Spirit, avoid grieving the Spirit, and continually yield themselves to His influence.

The Christian life cannot be lived successfully through human effort alone.

God has provided His Spirit to transform believers into the likeness of Christ and empower them for service.

The more we depend upon the Holy Spirit, the more Christ is reflected in our lives.
