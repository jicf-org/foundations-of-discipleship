# CHAPTER 11

# THE FUTURE

## Key Verse

Titus 2:13

> "Waiting for our blessed hope, the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ."

* * *

# Introduction

Every generation has wondered about the future.

Wars, natural disasters, political upheaval, economic uncertainty, and personal suffering often cause people to ask:

- What is happening in the world?
- Is history moving toward a purpose?
- Will evil ultimately triumph?
- What happens after death?
- Will Jesus return?

The Bible does not answer every question we might ask.

However, it provides everything we need to know in order to live with confidence, hope, and faithfulness.

Christians throughout history have differed on some details regarding the timing and sequence of future events.

Yet they have remained united on the most important truths.

The Christian hope is not found in a timeline.

It is found in a Person.

Jesus Christ.

The future belongs to Him.

* * *

# 1\. CHRIST WILL RETURN

## Scripture

Acts 1:9–11

(Read the passage.)

One of the clearest promises in Scripture is that Jesus Christ will return.

After His resurrection, Jesus ascended into heaven.

As the disciples watched, angels announced:

> "This Jesus ... will come in the same way as you saw him go into heaven."

The return of Christ is not a symbolic idea.

It is a real event.

The same Jesus who:

- Was born in Bethlehem
- Died on the cross
- Rose from the dead
- Ascended into heaven

will return.

The early Christians lived with this expectation.

The return of Christ provided comfort during persecution and motivation for faithful living.

Christ's return reminds us that history is not moving aimlessly.

God's purposes will be fulfilled.

# 2\. THE RESURRECTION OF THE DEAD

## Scripture

1 Corinthians 15

(Read the chapter.)

The resurrection of Jesus guarantees the future resurrection of believers.

Death is not the end.

The grave does not have the final word.

Because Christ rose, His followers will also rise.

The Bible teaches that believers will receive resurrected bodies fit for eternal life.

These bodies will no longer experience:

- Sin
- Sickness
- Pain
- Weakness
- Death

The resurrection reminds us that God's salvation includes the complete restoration of His people.

The Christian hope is not escape from creation.

It is the renewal of creation.

# 3\. GOD WILL JUDGE THE WORLD

## Scripture

2 Corinthians 5:10

(Read the passage.)

The Bible teaches that God is both loving and just.

Because God is just, evil will not ultimately go unanswered.

Every person will one day stand before God.

For some, the idea of judgment is frightening.

For believers, judgment also provides reassurance.

Many injustices in this world appear unresolved.

Many evil acts seem unpunished.

God sees everything.

Nothing escapes His attention.

Judgment reminds us that history matters.

Our choices matter.

Our lives matter.

God's justice will ultimately prevail.

# 4\. GOD WILL MAKE ALL THINGS NEW

## Scripture

Revelation 21:1–5

(Read the passage.)

The Bible does not end with destruction.

It ends with restoration.

John describes a new heaven and a new earth.

God's dwelling place will be with His people.

Sin will be gone.

Death will be defeated.

Suffering will cease.

Scripture describes a future where:

- Tears are wiped away.
- Death is no more.
- Mourning is no more.
- Pain is no more.

The Christian story begins in a garden.

It ends in a renewed creation.

God's plan is not merely to rescue individuals.

It is to renew all things.

This promise provides tremendous hope for believers living in a broken world.

# 5\. LIVE READY AND LIVE HOPEFULLY

## Scripture

Matthew 24:42–44

(Read the passage.)

Because Christ will return, believers are called to live with readiness.

Jesus repeatedly emphasized watchfulness.

However, biblical readiness is not fear.

It is faithfulness.

Many people become preoccupied with predicting dates and identifying signs.

Jesus emphasized something different.

He emphasized:

- Faithfulness
- Obedience
- Perseverance
- Readiness

The question is not:

"When will Christ return?"

The better question is:

"Will I be faithful when He returns?"

The New Testament consistently teaches that future hope should produce present obedience.

The believer who looks forward to Christ's return seeks to live in a way that honors Him today.

Hope inspires holiness.

Expectation motivates faithfulness.

# CHAPTER SUMMARY

The future belongs to God.

Christians do not place their hope in political systems, economic prosperity, technological advancement, or human progress.

Our hope is found in Jesus Christ.

The Bible teaches several truths about the future that every believer can confidently embrace:

- Jesus Christ will return.
- The dead will be raised.
- God will judge the world.
- Evil will be defeated.
- God will create a new heaven and a new earth.
- Believers will dwell with Him forever.

While Christians may disagree about certain prophetic details, these foundational truths unite believers across denominations and generations.

The purpose of biblical prophecy is not speculation.

It is preparation.

God reveals the future so that His people may live faithfully in the present.

Because Christ is coming again, believers are called to live with hope, holiness, perseverance, and readiness.

The Christian life is not driven by fear of the future.

It is driven by confidence in the One who holds the future.
